Sensline

Find the mouse curve that fits your hand.

A mouse curve keeps your sens steady when you aim slowly and speeds it up on fast swings, so small adjustments stay precise and big turns take less arm.

Sensline watches how you really aim in a short Valorant-style test, then builds the settings file for RawAccel, a free mouse acceleration driver for Windows.

Takes about 5 minutes. Runs in your browser and saves only on this device.

Step 1

Your mouse

Use what you play with today. You'll find your sens in Valorant under Settings, General, and your DPI in your mouse's software.

Step 2

Play the aim test

Play the way you do in a match. There are 24 enemies, and your normal sens is used so we can see how your hand really moves.

  • PeeksEnemies step out from cover
  • StrafesEnemies run side to side
  • FlicksEnemies stand off to one side
  • TurnsEnemies appear behind you

If RawAccel is already running with a curve, turn it off first. Otherwise the test applies acceleration twice.

Step 3

Your curve

The line shows your sens at each hand speed. Slow on the left, fast on the right.

Finish the aim test and your recommended curve appears here. You can also pick a starting point below.

      Step 4

      Install your curve

      In Valorant, set your sens to

      Versions older than 1.6 aren't supported, so update to the latest first.

      1. Get RawAccel from its official GitHub page if you don't have it.
      2. Close RawAccel, then back up the settings.json in its folder. Your new file replaces it, including any device settings and profiles.
      3. Download your file and put it in that folder.
      4. Open rawaccel.exe, check it matches the list below, then click Apply. Your curve isn't active until you do.
      5. To load it automatically each time you open the app, turn on Advanced, Apply Settings.json On GUI Startup.
      6. Keep your mouse at the DPI you entered in step 1. Acceleration and Input Offset are worked out for that DPI.

      If the app still shows Off after you open it, the file didn't fit your version and RawAccel replaced it without a warning. Check the version above and try again, or type the values in using the list below.

      RawAccel's FAQ says it's signed and works in Valorant, but also that anti-cheat decisions can't be guaranteed. I couldn't find a statement from Riot, so that risk is yours.

      Match these values in RawAccel

      These use the same names as the app, so you can check it line by line or type them in, then click Apply.

      Leave Sens Multiplier at 1. It scales every speed, including your slow aim, so Acceleration and Cap do the tuning here.

      Keep DPI at 0 in Device Menu. If you entered your mouse DPI there earlier, set it back to 0, otherwise everything feels slower.
